# Gold News Analyzer for Price Trend and Volatility Evaluation

**Status:** Research / development; not a verified production model  
**Area:** Financial NLP and quantitative data analysis  
**Technologies under study:** Python, FinBERT, NLP, machine learning, time-series features

## Goal

Study relationships between economic news, financial sentiment, gold price direction and volatility.

## Proposed research workflow

1. Collect legally permitted news information and align publication timestamps with price observations.
2. Explore sentiment labels (positive, neutral, negative) and quantitative market features.
3. Evaluate baseline approaches against machine learning candidates.
4. Use chronological evaluation, leakage prevention and appropriate uncertainty reporting.

## Disclosure policy

This page contains **no actual news collection, raw price history, private dataset, model weights, proprietary prompts, execution rules, access keys, or performance results**.

The project summary reflects research objectives, **not** an assertion that the system already achieves reliable direction or volatility predictions.
